# Script skeletons

Each episode names one of these in its front matter (skeleton: <name>). The next episode must use a different skeleton from the last two, so the channel never feels templated (YouTube's July 2026 guidance on repetitive content). The hook, open loops, villain and "Where you see it today" rules in prompts/script-gen.md apply to all of them.

## cold-open-crisis
Start inside the crisis (the tool failing, the deadline, the accident). Flash back to how it got there. Build the invention, its golden age, the rival that ends it, and come back to the crisis to close the loop.

## rivalry
Two materials or two people fighting for the same job. Alternate between them section by section. The turning point is the test, the court case or the war order that decides it. End with who really won and why.

## investigation
Open on a mystery object or a strange fact ("this tool is in every jet engine, and nobody knows its name"). Each section answers one question and raises the next. The final answer reframes the start.

## countdown
Built around a hard deadline or a race (a war production target, a record attempt, a patent expiring). Each section moves the clock forward. Stakes rise until the deadline, then the aftermath.

## life-of-an-object
Follow one physical object (a single tool bit, a valve, a saw blade) from ore to scrap. The big history happens around it. Good for showing the engineering at the cutting edge up close.
